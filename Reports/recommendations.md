# Recommendations

Each recommendation is linked to the insight in `business_insights.md` that supports it. These recommendations are drawn from complaint records only. They should be validated with internal operational data before action is taken.

Count > 30 days | Medium |

## What this analysis cannot tell you (limitations)
- **Company response or resolution time.** There is no company response date. The forwarding interval is CFPB-side only.
- **Why** timeliness dropped in 2021 or volume spiked in July 2023. Those causes need internal data.
- **Whether complaints were justified**, or whether consumers were satisfied with the response.
- **Complaint rates.** There are no customer or account counts, so volumes cannot be normalised by size.
